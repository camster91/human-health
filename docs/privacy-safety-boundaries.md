# Privacy and safety boundaries

Human Health is a fitness/health-support product, not a medical authority.

The product may:
- Track user-entered and connected health/fitness observations
- Provide general wellness and training recommendations
- Surface trends and encourage professional follow-up when appropriate

The product must not autonomously:
- Diagnose disease
- Prescribe medication or insulin doses
- Clear injuries for return to sport
- Present arbitrary scores as medical truth
- Hide the data used to generate consequential recommendations

Sensitive data should be minimized, encrypted where appropriate, exportable/deletable by the user, and excluded from analytics/logs unless strictly necessary.
